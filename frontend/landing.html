The Problem

Students are losing the ability to think critically

Not because of AI, but because of how they learn with it.

Only 29%

of Gen-Z students cite STEM jobs as their first career choice, despite 75% expressing academic interest

33%

of Singaporeans said they did not pursue a career in science because they thought they were “not smart enough”

Copy-paste dependency

Students default to copying from AI instead of struggling through problems. They get answers without building understanding.

Not enough guidance

There aren’t enough teachers to guide every student through critical learning moments — so most face them alone, and quit.

Lost confidence

Students who struggle alone start believing they’re “not smart enough” for coding — it’s not ability, it’s the lack of the right support.
